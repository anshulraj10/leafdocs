---
title: Dollar Signs
---

The basic plan costs $5 and the pro plan costs $10 per month.

Most add-ons are priced in the $5-$10 range.

International pricing is shown as US$5, ($10) after tax.

A stray $$ in the middle of a sentence should not open a math block.

A single $x$ stays plain text, since inline math is round brackets only.
